## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to organize sales data, answer business questions, summarize performance, and present findings in a dashboard. AI-assisted prompts were prepared to help structure the analysis, identify suitable Excel methods, and document the workflow.

The workbook contains a `SalesData` sheet with sample transactions, an `Analysis` sheet for the 10 business questions, a `Dashboard` sheet with KPIs and charts, and a `ReadMe` sheet with workbook notes.

## Project Objectives

The project explores these 10 business questions:

1. What are the total sales for each city?
2. Which are the top 5 cities by total sales?
3. What are the month-wise total sales?
4. Which product category has the highest sales?
5. Which payment method is used most frequently?
6. Which are the top 5 best-selling products?
7. What is the average customer age for each city?
8. How do sales compare between 2023 and 2024, including each year's contribution?
9. What is the distribution of customer ratings?
10. How do sales vary across customer age groups?

## Analysis Approach

The exact Excel method can vary by Excel version. Typical approaches include:

- `SUMIF` / `SUMIFS` to calculate sales totals by city, month, category, year, or age group.
- `COUNTIF` / `COUNTIFS` to count transactions or ratings.
- `AVERAGEIF` / `AVERAGEIFS` to calculate average customer age.
- Sorting and ranking summarized totals to find top-performing cities and products.
- PivotTables and charts to summarize and visualize results.

Open the workbook in Excel and allow formulas to recalculate. If results appear blank or stale, try **Ctrl + Alt + F9** to force a full recalculation.
